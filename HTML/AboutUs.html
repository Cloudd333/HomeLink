Our Story

Built on a Vision

Founded in 2020, HomeLink was born from a simple belief — that finding a home should feel as exciting as living in one. We set out to build a platform that combines premium property listings with a seamless, human-centred experience.

Today we serve buyers, sellers and renters across 12 South African cities, connecting thousands of people with properties that truly feel like home.

With a focus on customer satisfaction, cutting-edge technology and a passionate team of property professionals, HomeLink continues to redefine what a real estate platform can be.
